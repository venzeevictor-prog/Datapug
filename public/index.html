FAQ

Questions people ask before their first order

What is FlutterDataPlug?

FlutterDataPlug is a data bundle marketplace where you can buy cheap weekly, monthly, and 2-3 month data plans for MTN, Glo, Airtel, and 9mobile, all from one wallet-based dashboard.

How do I pay for data?

You fund an in-app wallet through Paystack (minimum ₦1,000), then buy plans against that balance. Every transaction — funding, order debit, referral bonus, or refund — is recorded in your transaction history.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders complete in seconds to minutes. If a network takes longer to confirm, your order shows as pending and resolves automatically — you can watch it update from Order history.

How does the referral program work?

Share your referral link from your dashboard's Referrals page. When someone signs up through it and funds their wallet with at least ₦1,000, you're automatically credited ₦3,500.
